# Genra demo script

## Three-minute walkthrough

**0:00–0:30 — The premise.** Open the landing page and let the hero establish the marketplace as a film rather than a dashboard. Point to the replaceable hero video treatment, the creator search pill, and the statement: “AI changed the tools. We changed the brief.”

**0:30–1:10 — Discover.** Select Creators. Search `Veo`, toggle Verified only, and open Mara Lennox. Call out the score ring, tool receipts, workflow timeline, licensing language, and portfolio lightbox. The point is that taste and process are searchable, not hidden in a pitch deck.

**1:10–2:00 — Brief it.** Go to Post a Brief. Paste “A surreal 30-second film for a new fragrance launch — glossy, intimate, a little strange.” Click Build my brief. Show the structured title, type, style, formats, and live preview. Publish and land on the brief detail page.

**2:00–2:40 — Match.** Use the match rail to explain the transparent score: tool overlap, format coverage, verification, and invite. Open one creator from the rail and send an invite. The toast confirms the engagement state without needing real auth.

**2:40–3:00 — Switch roles.** Toggle Creator in the navbar and open Creator studio. Show profile strength, invitations, and toolkit verification. Close on the idea that Genra makes AI-native craft legible from first discovery through delivery.

## Six slide outline

1. **Problem:** Great AI work is distributed, and brand teams lack a trustworthy way to evaluate process.
2. **Approach:** Genra combines creator discovery, structured briefs, visible verification, and explainable matching.
3. **Profiles:** Portfolio evidence is paired with tools, models, workflow steps, licensing, and commercial terms.
4. **Search:** Facets expose specialization, tool overlap, formats, availability, ratings, and verification.
5. **Brief builder:** A rough idea becomes an editable, structured creative brief with a live preview.
6. **Verification + roadmap:** Tool, workflow, and past-work signals create trust now; future roadmap adds richer collaboration, provenance receipts, and delivery handoffs.
